Free Asset Resources

Curated, high-quality free assets to test digital twins: unwrapped 3D models and multi-map PBR fabric sets (Diffuse/Normal/Roughness).

Poly Haven — Models (GLB/GLTF)

High-quality, CC0 furniture models (sofas, chairs) that are often already UV-unwrapped and ready for fabric textures.

Format: .glb/.gltf · License: CC0

Sketchfab — Free & Downloadable

Search for "sofa" or "chair" and filter by "Downloadable" and license (CC0) to find ready-to-use models.

Khronos — Sample Models

Official glTF sample models such as SheenChair and Couch—perfect for testing fabric maps and GLTF/GLB loaders.

Poly Haven — Fabrics (PBR Sets)

Professional-grade scans (denim, linen, leather) — download ZIPs containing Diffuse/Normal/Roughness maps for realistic materials.

AmbientCG — Cloth Textures
